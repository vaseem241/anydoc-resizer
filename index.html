Frequently Asked Questions

Can I customize the width, height, and target KB freely? ▼

Yes. By default, "Custom Dimensions & File Size" is selected. You can type any exact width, height, maximum KB, or minimum KB required by your specific portal or employer.

Are documents and files uploaded to your servers during conversion? ▼

No. AnyDoc Resizer converts Word, PDF, and image documents entirely in your local browser session. Your documents, resumes, or marksheet scans never touch any remote server.

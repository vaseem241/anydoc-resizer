Frequently Asked Questions

Why do application portals reject uploaded photos? ▼

Most portals automatically scan file size (KB) and dimensions (width × height) before saving. If an image is 1 KB above the maximum allowed limit or slightly stretched, the submission gets rejected instantly.

Are my personal images uploaded to any server? ▼

No. AnyDoc Resizer processes all images locally inside your web browser via HTML5 Canvas. Your documents and photos never leave your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this tool compress photos to 20KB without blur? ▼

Most compressors simply reduce image resolution, resulting in blurry, pixelated faces. AnyDoc Resizer preserves the exact required pixel geometry and applies binary-search quantization to achieve optimal clarity right under the portal limit.

Are my personal images uploaded to any server? ▼

No. AnyDoc Resizer executes 100% locally in your web browser via HTML5 Canvas. Your documents, photos, and signatures never touch any remote server.

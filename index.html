Frequently Asked Questions

Why do online portals reject uploaded photos? ▼

Most portals automatically scan file size (KB) and dimensions (width × height) before saving. If an image is even 1 KB above the maximum allowed limit or slightly stretched, the submission gets rejected instantly.

How does this tool compress photos to 20KB without blur? ▼

Standard compressors simply lower the image resolution, resulting in blurry, pixelated faces. AnyDoc Resizer preserves the exact required pixel geometry and uses iterative binary-search quantization to achieve optimal sharpness right under the portal limit.

Are my personal images uploaded to any server? ▼

No. AnyDoc Resizer processes all images and documents entirely within your local browser session via HTML5 Canvas and client-side web workers. Your documents and photos never touch any external server.

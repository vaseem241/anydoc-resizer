Frequently Asked Questions

Why do Police, Army, and SSC portals reject photos? ▼

Portals automatically scan image file byte counts and aspect ratios before uploading. If your photo is even 1 KB above 50 KB or 1 pixel off from the specified 200x230 ratio, automated portal scripts immediately reject the submission.

Are my personal photos or signatures saved to any server? ▼

No. This tool operates 100% locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your image is never uploaded to any remote server or stored in any database.
